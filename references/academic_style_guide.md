# Academic Style Guide: Technical Formatting & Typography Standards

Technical formatting standards for academic theses, dissertations, research proposals, and scientific journal submissions. Aligned with international academic standards (APA 7th Edition & IEEE).

---

## 1. Page Setup & Document Layout

* **Paper Size:** A4 (21.0 cm x 29.7 cm), standard 80 gsm paper.
* **Printing Format:** Double-sided printing from Chapter 1 through appendices.
* **Document Margins:**
  * **Left Margin:** 4.0 cm (accommodates bookbinding / binding margin).
  * **Top Margin:** 2.5 cm.
  * **Right Margin:** 2.5 cm.
  * **Bottom Margin:** 2.5 cm.
* **Title Page (Cover):** Title typography begins **4 blank lines** down from the top margin.
* **Institutional Integrity:**
  * No blank pages permitted mid-manuscript, except required structural section dividers.
  * Never leave orphan headings or table labels at the bottom line of a page (*keep-with-next enforcement*).

---

## 2. Typography & Font Hierarchy

* **Primary Typeface:** **Times New Roman (TNR)** across all document sections.
* **Font Color:** **100% Pure Black (`#000000` / RGB 0, 0, 0)**. Web clipboard gray tints (`#374151`) are strictly forbidden.
* **Primary Line Spacing:** **1.5 Line Spacing** (*Spacing Before = 0 pt, Spacing After = 0 pt*).
* **Text Alignment:** **Justified (Rata Kanan-Kiri)**.
  * *Strict restriction:* Never insert manual double-spaces between words to force alignment.
* **Paragraph Indentation:** First line of every body paragraph indented by **1.0 cm** (or 0.5 in / 1.27 cm). Do not insert extra blank paragraph returns between regular paragraphs.

### Type Size & Weight Matrix

| Manuscript Component | Typeface | Size | Weight & Formatting | Alignment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Article / Document Title** | Times New Roman | 12 pt | **Bold, ALL CAPS** | Center |
| **Chapter Heading (Level 1)** | Times New Roman | 12 pt | **Bold, ALL CAPS** | Center |
| **Sub-heading (Level 2)** | Times New Roman | 12 pt | **Bold**, Title Case | Left |
| **Sub-sub-heading (Level 3)** | Times New Roman | 12 pt | **Bold-Italic**, Title Case | Left |
| **Body Text** | Times New Roman | 12 pt | Regular | Justified |
| **Table Title & Header** | Times New Roman | 12 pt | Regular (Title: Title Case) | Left / Center |
| **Table Data Cells** | Times New Roman | 10–12 pt | Regular (Numeric: Right, Text: Left) | Consistent |
| **Figure Captions** | Times New Roman | 12 pt | Regular (Title Case) | Center (Below figure) |
| **Foreign & Technical Terms** | Times New Roman | 12 pt | *Italics* | Follows body text |

---

## 3. Bold & Italic Rules

* **Mandatory Italics:**
  1. Non-standard, foreign, or Latin technical terminology (e.g., *machine learning*, *ground truth*, *dataset*, *outlier*, *framework*, *preprocessing*, *in vivo*, *Staphylococcus aureus*).
  2. Direct qualitative interview quotes within narrative text.
  3. Titles of books, journals, and periodicals in the References section.
* **Bold Restrictions:**
  * Bold weight is **strictly reserved** for: Document Titles, Chapter Headings, Section Sub-headings, and Table/Figure Numbers (`**Table 3.1**`).
  * **Never bold text inside body paragraphs.**

---

## 4. Scientific Tables (APA 7th Style)

* Table titles are positioned **above the table**, left-aligned or centered: `Table X.X Title of Table` (Title Case).
* **The 3-Line Open Format:**
  * **Line 1:** Top horizontal border.
  * **Line 2:** Horizontal divider beneath column headers.
  * **Line 3:** Bottom horizontal border closing the table.
  * **Vertical column borders are strictly prohibited.**
* Cell spacing: 1.0 line spacing inside table cells.

---

## 5. Figures & Diagrams

* Figure captions are positioned **below the figure**, centered: `Figure X.X Caption of Figure`.
* Minimum spacing: 1 blank line between figure and caption; 2 blank lines between caption and subsequent narrative prose.

---

## 6. References & Citations (APA 7th Edition)

* In-text citations follow Author-Date format: *(Smith & Doe, 2023)* or *Smith et al. (2024)* for three or more authors.
* Reference list entries formatted with a 1.27 cm hanging indent, alphabetized by first author's surname.
